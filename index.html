Essential Cat Care Guide

Cats are obligate carnivores requiring animal protein. Ensure access to fresh water daily, and consider a water fountain since cats prefer moving water.

Provide daily interactive play sessions with wand toys to mimic hunting. Scratching posts are essential to keep claws healthy and protect your furniture.

Regular brushing reduces hairballs, especially for longhaired breeds. Schedule annual veterinary check-ups to keep vaccinations and dental health up to date.
